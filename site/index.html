Why maintainers use it

Codeberg, Godot, OpenJDK, Rust, and Ghostty all restricted AI-generated contributions this year, and enforcing a policy means someone has to read every submission twice. SlopScreen does the first read: a scan button on every PR and issue, a badge on every comment, and a breakdown of exactly which signals fired so you can judge for yourself.

Honest by design

The classifier is calibrated to a 5% false-positive operating point and abstains on text too short to judge. Every result is labeled a signal, not a verdict. Flagging a real person's writing as AI is worse than missing one bot, and the tool is built around that.

Private by design

Nothing leaves your browser until you click scan. Scanned text is scored and discarded, never stored. No accounts, no tracking. The whole policy fits on one page.
